Mouse Controller Privacy Policy

Download on theApp Store Download on theMac App Store
OverviewSupportPrivacy PolicyStory

Mouse Controller does not collect, store, or share any personal data. There are no accounts, servers, ads or analytics.

How the Apps Communicate

  • The iPhone app and the Mac app talk directly to each other over your local network. No internet connection is needed.
  • The connection is encrypted with a key derived from your pairing code, so others on the same network can't see what you type or control your Mac.
  • Only control commands are sent, such as cursor movement, clicks, keys and volume.

Data Stored by the App

  • iPhone: the Macs you have paired with, and your settings such as pointer speed, acceleration, haptics and gestures. They stay on your iPhone.
  • Mac: the pairing code and the paired iPhones. They stay on your Mac.

Sending the Mac App Link

If you use the button that sends the Mac app's Mac App Store link with AirDrop, the link goes only to the device you choose. Mouse Controller does not see or store it.

Permissions

  • iPhone: Local Network, to find and connect to your Mac.
  • Mac: Accessibility, to move the cursor, click and type on your behalf.

The App Does Not

  • Collect personal information
  • Track users
  • Use analytics or advertising services
  • Send data to slon3studio or any external server
  • Store a history of what you type or do
