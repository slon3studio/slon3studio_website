How Tačka was made

Tačka (Slovenian for “little paw”) began as a plan for a dog walking app made for Slovenia, where the big international marketplaces didn't appear to operate. It connects dog owners with ID-verified walkers and sitters, and shows every walk live.

Built for Slovenia

The app is in Slovenian, with English as a second language, and it covers the whole of Slovenia. A small market also means the walkers can be met and supported in person.

Trust comes first

Letting a stranger walk your dog takes trust. Every walker verifies their identity with an ID document and a selfie, through our partner Didit, before owners can see them. The map shows approximate areas, never home addresses, and reports about profiles or reviews are checked by hand.

Every walk, live

When the walker taps Start, the owner sees a running timer and the route drawing on the map. GPS runs only while the walk is on, and when it ends the owner gets a report with the route, duration, photos and a note.

Free, with no commission

The first plan had payments in the app and a commission. The version that shipped is simpler: Tačka is free, takes no commission, and owners pay the walker directly, in cash or by bank transfer.
