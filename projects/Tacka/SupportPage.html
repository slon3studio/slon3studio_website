Need help with Tačka, want to report a problem with a walk, or have an idea for the app? We're happy to help.

Requirements

  • iPhone running iOS 17 or later
  • An internet connection
  • You must be 18 or older to use Tačka
  • Walkers need location access, so the owner can follow the walk

Common Questions

  • How do I sign up? With Sign in with Apple, or with your email and a password. Pick whether you are an owner or a walker, go through the short guide, and fill in your profile.
  • How do I pay the walker? Tačka is free and has no payments in the app. Once a request is accepted, you see each other's phone number and agree on payment by cash or bank transfer. Tačka takes no commission.
  • How do I book? Open a walker's profile and tap Book. Pick who and what, then when (once or every week), and confirm. The walker accepts or declines the request in the app.
  • Why can't I see a walker's drop-in visits or home sitting? These services unlock for a walker after five completed bookings with a rating of at least 4.5.
  • How does the identity check work? Walkers verify their identity with a photo of an ID document and a selfie, checked by our partner Didit. Only verified walkers are shown to owners. Tačka keeps no copy of the document.
  • When can the owner see my location? Only between Start and Stop of a walk, and only the owner of that booking. It stops on its own when you tap Stop.
  • When can I start a walk? From 30 minutes before its booked time.
  • The route isn't drawing on the map. Allow location for Tačka in Settings. Choose “Always” so the route keeps recording while your phone is locked.
  • Who can see my address and phone number? Other users see only your first name, the initial of your last name, your photo and your area. Your phone number and full name are shown only to the person you have a confirmed booking with.
  • Can I be both an owner and a walker? Yes. You can switch between owner and walker mode in your profile.
  • Someone behaved badly. Use Report on their profile or review, or block them from the ••• menu on their profile. Reports are reviewed by hand. You can also write to us below.
  • How do I delete my account? In the app, go to Profile, tap the gear icon at the top right and choose Delete account. Your profile, pets, bookings, walk routes and photos are deleted. A walk that is running has to be finished first.
