Need help with Rotera or have an idea for the app? We're happy to help.

Requirements

  • An iPhone for the app, or any modern web browser for the web app at rotera.slon3studio.si (including on Android phones)
  • An internet connection, since the schedule is shared with your team
  • An email address or an Apple Account to create your account

Common Questions

  • To join your team, ask your manager for the organization's join code and enter it when you sign up. With Sign in with Apple, choose "I have a code" after signing in.
  • To start a new team, choose "I run a team". A short walkthrough helps you pick your line of work, which fills in the shift times, and share the join code.
  • Managers find and share the join code in the Team tab. Changing it stops the old code from working.
  • On Android, open the Rotera web app in Chrome and choose Install app (or Add to Home screen) so it opens like a regular app.
  • Staff see their own week first under My shifts. The whole team's schedule is under Full schedule.
  • Submit your wishes for next week in the Wishes tab: morning, afternoon, any or off for each day, before the deadline shown above the list.
  • A cover hands your shift to a colleague. A trade swaps two shifts, so you both still work. Both need the manager's approval.
  • Building a schedule from wishes keeps the shifts the manager placed by hand, follows how many people each shift needs, and respects each person's weekly cap.
  • While editing the schedule, managers can hold a person and drag them to another shift or day. Revert puts the week back as it was.
  • Not getting notifications? Allow notifications for Rotera in the iPhone Settings app, and check which kinds are on in Settings → Notifications in the app.
  • Your worked hours follow the published schedule. If you corrected an entry yourself, it stays as you wrote it.
  • To change the language, open Settings → Language. It changes straight away.
  • To delete your account, open Profile (or Team), tap the gear icon, open Settings and choose Delete account at the bottom. See the Privacy Policy for what gets deleted.
