Who Provides Rotera

Rotera is made and run by slon3studio (Ivo Peterka s.p.), Slovenia. Contact: slon3studio@gmail.com.

The Service

Rotera helps teams that work in shifts plan their week: staff send their wishes, managers build and publish the schedule, and covers and trades happen in the app. We keep improving Rotera, so features may change over time.

Accounts and Organizations

  • You need an account to use Rotera. Keep your sign-in details safe and give correct information.
  • A manager creates an organization and is responsible for it: who joins with the join code, the schedule, the settings and the plan.
  • Staff join an organization with its join code. A manager can remove members from the team.
  • You must be old enough to work in your country to use Rotera.

Plans and Payment

  • Rotera is free for organizations of up to 5 active members. Bigger organizations need a paid plan, chosen by team size: Starter (up to 10), Team (up to 20), Business (up to 50) or Enterprise (up to 150), billed monthly or yearly. Larger teams can contact us.
  • A plan covers the whole organization. Only the manager pays; staff never pay.
  • In the iPhone app, plans are sold by Apple as auto-renewable subscriptions through the App Store. Payment is charged to the buyer's Apple Account. Apple's terms and Apple's refund process apply to these purchases.
  • A paid plan may start with a free month. If you do not cancel at least 24 hours before the free month ends, the plan continues and is charged.
  • Subscriptions renew automatically at the end of each period unless cancelled at least 24 hours before it ends. You can manage or cancel a subscription in your Apple Account settings, or under Manage subscription in the app. A cancelled plan stays active until the end of the period already paid.
  • Prices are shown in the app before you buy and include VAT where it applies. We may change prices for future periods; Apple tells you about a price change before it applies to you.
  • When a trial or paid plan ends and the organization has more than 5 active members, the organization becomes read-only: everyone can still see the schedule and their data, but changes need a plan again. Nothing is deleted because a plan ended.

Ending Your Use

  • Deleting your account does not cancel an App Store subscription. If you are the only manager and the subscription still renews, the app asks you to cancel it first.
