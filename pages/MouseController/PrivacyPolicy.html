Remote Mouse Privacy Policy

OverviewSupportPrivacy Policy

Remote Mouse does not collect, store, or share any personal data. There are no accounts, servers, ads or analytics.

How the Apps Communicate

  • The iPhone app and the Mac app talk directly to each other over your local network. No internet connection is needed.
  • The connection is encrypted with a key derived from your pairing code, so others on the same network can't see what you type or control your Mac.
  • Only control commands are sent, such as cursor movement, clicks, keys and volume.

Permissions

  • iPhone: Local Network, to find and connect to your Mac.
  • Mac: Accessibility, to move the cursor, click and type on your behalf.

The App Does Not

  • Collect personal information
  • Track users
  • Use analytics or advertising services
  • Send data to slon3studio or any external server
  • Store a history of what you type or do
