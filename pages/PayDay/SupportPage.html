Need help with PayDay or have an idea for the app? We're happy to help.

Requirements

  • iPhone running iOS 17 or later
  • An iCloud account if you want your data to sync between devices

Common Questions

  • To add a job, open the Profile tab and add it there. Each job gets its own tab.
  • You can log a shift for any past date if you forgot to add it on the day.
  • A check-out time earlier than the check-in time counts as an overnight shift.
  • If your data isn't syncing, make sure you're signed in to iCloud and iCloud is turned on for PayDay in Settings.
  • Deleting a shift always asks for confirmation first.
