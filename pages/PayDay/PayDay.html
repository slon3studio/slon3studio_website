Know your pay before payday

PayDay is a simple shift and pay tracker for iPhone. Add each job you work with its hourly rate, log your shifts, and PayDay works out your hours, earnings and tips, then projects what the whole month will pay.

Features

  • Log a shift in seconds
  • Repeat your last shift with one tap
  • As many jobs as you like, each with its own rate
  • Tip tracking for waiting and bar jobs
  • Projected pay for the current month
  • Monthly goals in hours or money
  • Calendar heatmap of the days you worked
  • Mark months paid and spot differences
  • Export timesheets as CSV or PDF
  • Sync across your devices with iCloud

How it works

Add a job with its hourly rate and usual hours, then log each shift as you work it. Missed one? Add it for any past date, or repeat your last shift with a tap. PayDay keeps the totals, averages and month projection up to date as you go.

Built with

  • SwiftUI
