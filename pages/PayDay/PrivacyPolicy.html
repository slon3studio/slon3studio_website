PayDay Privacy Policy

OverviewSupportPrivacy Policy

PayDay does not collect, sell, or share any personal data. There are no accounts, ads, or analytics in the app.

Data Stored by the App

  • Jobs you add, with their names, hourly rates and goals
  • Shifts you log, including times, tips and notes
  • Payments you mark as received
  • Your app settings, such as currency and appearance

Where Your Data Lives

  • On your iPhone
  • In your own private iCloud database, if iCloud is turned on, so it can sync between your devices

Data in your private iCloud database is only accessible to you and is handled under Apple's privacy policy. slon3studio cannot see it.

The App Does Not

  • Collect personal information
  • Track users
  • Use analytics or advertising services
  • Send data to slon3studio or any third-party server

Exports

When you export a timesheet as CSV or PDF, the file is created on your device and only shared where you choose to send it.
