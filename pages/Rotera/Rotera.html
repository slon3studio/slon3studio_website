Rotera

The week's schedule, in one place

Rotera is a shift scheduling app for teams. The manager builds the weekly schedule, and everyone on the team sees their shifts, sends their wishes, asks for cover and trades shifts with colleagues, all from their phone or browser.

Features

  • Weekly schedule as a week grid or a day-by-day view
  • Employees send wishes for the shifts they want
  • Build a draft schedule from everyone's wishes
  • Copy last week's schedule in one tap
  • Publish the week when it's ready
  • Warnings when a shift clashes with another
  • Ask a colleague to cover a shift, approved by the manager
  • Trade shifts with a colleague, agreed by both and the manager
  • Your own positions, duties and shift times
  • Log the hours you worked and see your earnings
  • Optional tip tracking, switched on per team
  • Light and dark appearance

How it works

The manager creates an organization and shares its join code. Employees sign up with the code and land on the same schedule. Each week the manager collects wishes, builds the schedule, adjusts it by hand and publishes it. When plans change, a cover or a shift trade goes to the manager for approval and the schedule updates for everyone.

Everywhere your team is

Rotera is an iPhone app. On Android, or any computer, use the Rotera web app in your browser and add it to your home screen so it opens like a regular app. The app is currently in Slovenian.
