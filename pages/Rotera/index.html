Rotera

Shift scheduling for any team that works in shifts, from restaurants, cafés and shops to care homes and warehouses.

The week's schedule, in one place

Rotera is a shift scheduling app for teams. Staff say when they can work, the manager builds the week, and everyone sees exactly when they work. Covers and shift trades happen in the app, and nobody has to chase anyone over chat or paper.

Screenshots

Features

  • Staff open the app on their own week: next shift, hours and every day at a glance
  • The whole team's schedule one tap away
  • Send wishes for each day: morning, afternoon, either or off
  • A wish deadline with a countdown the team can see
  • Managers see who can work and where people are still missing
  • Draft the week from wishes, following how many people each shift needs
  • A personal cap on shifts per week for each team member
  • Drag people between shifts and days while editing
  • Copy last week's schedule, then publish the week when it's ready
  • Print the week or save it as a PDF
  • Covers and shift trades, approved by the manager
  • Notifications for covers, trades, a published schedule and the wish deadline
  • Add your shifts to your iPhone calendar
  • Log the hours you worked and see your earnings, with optional tips
  • Sign in with email or with Apple
  • Profile pictures, light and dark mode
  • In English, Slovenian and German

How it works

The manager creates an organization, picks their line of work to fill in the shift times, and shares the join code. Staff sign up with the code, by email or with Apple, and land on the same schedule. Each week staff send their wishes before the deadline, and the manager drafts the schedule from them, adjusts it by hand and publishes it. When plans change, a cover or a shift trade goes to the manager for approval and the schedule updates for everyone.

Everywhere your team is

Rotera is an iPhone app. On Android, or any computer, use the Rotera web app in your browser and add it to your home screen so it opens like a regular app. Rotera is available in English, Slovenian and German.

Requirements

  • iPhone, or any modern browser for the web app, Android included
  • An internet connection, since the schedule is shared
  • The join code from your manager, or start your own team
  • An email address or an Apple Account
