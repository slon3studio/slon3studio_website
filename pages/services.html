Questions

Good to know

What does “from” mean?

The price covers the package exactly as described. If you need more pages, screens or features, you get a written quote with the final price before any work starts, and it doesn't change unless you ask for more.

How do I pay?

Half when we start and half at launch. For custom projects, you pay stage by stage.

Who owns the website or app?

You do. The domain, the App Store listing and the source code are in your name.

What are the running costs?

For a website, usually only the domain, about €10 to €20 a year. For an iPhone app, Apple's developer fee of 99 USD a year. Apps with a backend add server costs, usually €0 to €25 a month.

Can I change texts myself later?

Send us the change and we make it, which is included in the Care plan. Without it, small changes are billed by the hour.
