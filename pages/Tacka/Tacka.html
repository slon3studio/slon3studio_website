Your dog in good hands

Tačka (Slovenian for “little paw”) connects dog owners with dog walkers and pet sitters nearby. Owners find someone they trust, send a request, and follow every walk live on a map. Walkers get a profile, a schedule and new clients in their neighbourhood. Tačka starts in Ljubljana and is available in Slovenian and English.

For dog owners

  • Find walkers and sitters near you on a list or a map
  • Filter by service, price and who is available today
  • Profiles with photo, bio, prices, reviews and completed walks
  • Walks, drop-in visits and home sitting
  • One-off or recurring bookings, such as Mon, Wed and Fri at noon
  • Pet profiles with size, temperament, muzzle and vet notes
  • Watch the walk live: timer, the walker's position and the route
  • Walk report with route, photos, pee and poo ticks and a note
  • Rate and review after every completed walk

For walkers and sitters

  • Public profile with your services, prices and area
  • Weekly availability and a calendar of upcoming walks
  • Accept or decline requests
  • Leash and muzzle check before every walk
  • One tap Start and Stop, with GPS only while the walk runs
  • Add photos, ticks and a note during the walk
  • Checklist for sittings: water, food and meds
  • History of every walk you have done

How it works

The owner picks a walker and sends a request for a time and a dog. Once the walker accepts, both see each other's phone number and agree on payment between themselves, by cash or bank transfer. The first version of Tačka is free, with no payments in the app. When the walker taps Start, the owner sees a running timer and the walker's live route on the map. Location sharing stops on its own when the walker taps Stop, and the owner gets the walk report.

Requirements

  • iPhone with iOS 17 or later
  • You must be 18 or older
  • Starting in Ljubljana
  • Walkers need location access during a walk
