Tačka connects dog owners with dog walkers and sitters, so it needs some personal data to work. This policy explains what is collected, why, and who can see it. Tačka does not sell your data, show ads, or use tracking or analytics services.

Who Is Responsible

Tačka is made by slon3studio, Slovenia. For any privacy question, write to slon3studio@gmail.com.

Data We Collect

  • Account: first and last name, email address and password (stored hashed, we never see it). If you use Sign in with Apple, we receive the name and email Apple shares with us.
  • Contact and address: phone number and your home address with its map position, used to show walkers or owners near you.
  • Age confirmation: that you are 18 or older, and when you accepted the Terms.
  • Profile: profile photo, bio, and for walkers your services, prices, area, service radius, accepted dog sizes, languages and weekly availability.
  • Pets: name, breed, size, age, temperament, whether a muzzle is needed, a photo, and optional notes such as key or door instructions and vet contact.
  • Bookings: times, services, prices, notes and booking status.
  • Walks: start and end time, the walker's GPS route, photos taken during the walk, ticks (pee, poo, food, meds) and a note.
  • Reviews, reports and blocks you make in the app.

Location

Tačka uses the walker's location only between Start and Stop of a walk or visit. During that time the route is saved and shared live with the owner of that booking, including while the phone is locked. Location tracking stops automatically at Stop and is never collected at any other time. Owners' location is not tracked; the app only uses the address you enter to find walkers near you.

Who Can See Your Data

  • Other users see your public profile: first name and last initial, photo, area (not your exact address), bio, services and reviews.
  • The other person in a confirmed booking also sees your full name and phone number. A walker sees a pet's private notes (such as key instructions and vet contact) only after accepting the booking.
  • Only the owner of a booking sees the live walk, the route and the walk photos.
  • Service providers that run the app for us: Supabase (database, sign-in and photo storage, hosted in the EU) and Apple (Sign in with Apple). They process data only on our behalf.
  • Authorities, only when the law requires it.

Data Stored on Your iPhone

  • Whether you are in owner or walker mode, and your light or dark appearance setting
  • Your sign-in session, so you stay signed in

Payments

The current version of Tačka has no payments in the app and collects no card or bank details. Owners and walkers agree on payment themselves.

How Long We Keep Data

  • Account and profile data: while you have an account.
  • GPS routes of walks: 90 days, unless a report or dispute about that walk is still open.
  • Reviews: stay after you delete your account, but anonymised, so they are no longer linked to you.

You can delete your account at any time in the app (Profile → Delete account). This deletes your profile, address, phone number, pets, bookings, walk routes and photos.

Your Rights

Under the GDPR you can ask to access, correct, delete or export your data, or object to how it is used. Write to us and we'll reply within 30 days. You can also complain to the Slovenian Information Commissioner (Informacijski pooblaščenec, www.ip-rs.si).

Security

Data is sent over encrypted connections (HTTPS), and database rules let each user read and change only what they are allowed to.

Children

Tačka is only for people aged 18 and over. We do not knowingly collect data from children.

The App Does Not

  • Track your location outside an active walk
  • Sell or share your data for advertising
  • Use analytics or advertising services
  • Track you across other apps or websites
