App

Slippy

Scan your card slips and receipts at the end of a shift and let Slippy add them up.

OverviewSupportPrivacy Policy

Cash up in seconds

Slippy is a calculator for waiters and cashiers. Hold a card slip or receipt up to the camera, and Slippy reads the final amount and adds it to your total. No more typing in every slip by hand at the end of a long shift.

Features

  • Full-screen camera with a frame to line up the slip
  • Reads the final amount, not just the biggest number
  • Understands totals in Slovenian, English, Croatian, German and Italian
  • Adds the amount automatically once the reading is steady
  • Manual mode: confirm each amount with one tap
  • Flashlight for dark bars and back rooms
  • Full calculator with brackets and a live result
  • Tap any amount to edit or delete it
  • Scanned amounts are marked apart from typed ones
  • Your running total is kept when you close the app

How it works

Point the camera at a slip and line it up in the frame. Slippy reads only the text inside the frame, scores every number it finds by keywords like TOTAL or SKUPAJ, currency and position, and skips dates, percentages, VAT and subtotals. When the same amount is read a few frames in a row, it's added to your sum with a tap of haptic feedback. Move the slip away and the next one is ready to scan, even if it has the same amount.
