OverviewSupportPrivacy Policy

Slippy does not collect, sell, or share any personal data. There are no accounts, ads, or analytics in the app.

Camera

Slippy uses the camera only to read the amount on the slip you hold up. The text is recognized on your iPhone with Apple's Vision framework. Camera images are not saved and never leave your device.

Data Stored by the App

  • Your current calculation, so it's still there when you reopen the app

This is stored only on your iPhone. Clearing the calculator removes it.

The App Does Not

  • Collect personal information
  • Save photos of your slips
  • Track users
  • Use analytics or advertising services
  • Send data to slon3studio or any third-party server
