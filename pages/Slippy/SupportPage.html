Slippy Support

Need help with Slippy or have an idea for the app? We're happy to help.

Requirements

  • iPhone running iOS 17 or later
  • Camera access, to scan slips

Common Questions

  • If Slippy can't open the camera, allow camera access for Slippy in Settings.
  • Hold the slip flat and inside the frame. In a dark room, turn on the flashlight.
  • To scan two slips with the same amount, move the first one out of view before showing the second.
  • If you'd rather check each amount first, switch from automatic to manual and tap Add.
  • Read a wrong amount? Tap it in the sum to edit or delete it.
  • You can always type amounts in by hand with the keypad.
