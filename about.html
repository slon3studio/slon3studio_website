About

Hi, we're slon3studio

An independent web and app studio from Slovenia.

slon3studio is a Slovenian studio for websites and mobile apps. We started by designing, building and publishing our own apps, and five of them are live on the App Store today. Now we bring the same care to websites and apps for businesses.

Every project is planned, designed, built and supported in-house, with a fixed price agreed before any work starts. We focus on clear design, fast websites and apps that are simple to use, and from the first sketch to launch and beyond you always know what comes next and what it costs.

"Slon" is Slovenian for elephant, which is where the elephant in our logo comes from.

  • HTML & CSS
  • JavaScript & TypeScript
  • React
  • Responsive design
  • SEO & Google Search Console
  • Multilingual websites
  • Cookie-free analytics
  • Swift & SwiftUI
  • React Native & Expo

Mobile apps are built with React Native and Expo: one codebase for both iPhone and Android, published on the App Store and Google Play.
