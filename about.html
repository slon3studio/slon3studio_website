About

Hi, we're slon3studio

An independent web and app studio from Slovenia.

slon3studio is a one-person studio. The developer behind it loves apps, web design and game development, and with a degree in Mathematics and Computer Science brings logic and creativity to everything from iPhone apps and Unity games to responsive websites.

"Slon" means elephant, which is where the elephant in our logo comes from.

  • Supabase: databases and sign-in
  • Vercel & GitHub Pages hosting
  • Cookie-free analytics
  • Swift & SwiftUI
  • React Native & Expo

Mobile apps are built with React Native and Expo: one codebase for both iPhone and Android, published on the App Store and Google Play.
