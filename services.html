Questions

Good to know

Do I need to prepare anything?

No. If you have a logo, texts or photos, send them. Everything else, including the domain, hosting and store accounts, we arrange for you.

What does “from” mean?

The price covers the package exactly as described. If you need more pages, screens or features, you get a written quote with the final price before any work starts, and it doesn't change unless you ask for more.

How do I pay?

Half when we start and half at launch. For custom projects, you pay stage by stage.

Who owns the website or app?

You get the full source code. The domain and the store listings can be in your name, or we manage them for you and transfer them whenever you ask.

What are the running costs?

None in the first year: the domain, hosting and store fees are included in the price until the first renewal. After that, the Care plan covers them, or you pay the renewals yourself, for example about €10 to €20 a year for a domain.

Can I change texts myself later?

Send us the change and we make it, which is included in the Care plan. Without it, small changes are billed by the hour.
